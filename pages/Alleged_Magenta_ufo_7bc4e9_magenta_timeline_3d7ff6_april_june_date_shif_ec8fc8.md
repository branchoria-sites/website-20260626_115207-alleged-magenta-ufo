## Introduction

The reason the [alleged Magenta UFO]({{ 'alleged-magenta-ufo/' | relative_url }}) incident is dated both **11 April 1933** and **13 June 1933** is not that two separate well-documented events took place. Rather, the public story developed over time from a small body of disputed documents and [later retellings]({{ 'later-retellings/' | relative_url }}) that do not consistently distinguish between an alleged sighting, a landing or crash, a military recovery, and subsequent government actions. As the story spread through UFO literature and later media coverage, many authors adopted one date while omitting the other, leaving readers with the impression of a single fixed timeline when none exists in the surviving public record.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.metabunk.org/threads/claim-a-ufo-crashed-in-1933-italy-and-the-us-recovered-it.13282/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: metabunk.org">[Metabunk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">metabunk.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">claim a ufo crashed in 1933 italy and the us recovered it.13282</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Claim: A UFO crashed in 1933 Italy and the US Recovered it15 Dec 2023 — While the Italian UFO crash story has been circulating ar...</span></span></span>

The shift from April to June therefore illustrates a broader issue with the Magenta narrative: its chronology depends heavily on later interpretation rather than on an independently verified contemporary record. Understanding why the dates differ helps explain why the [timeline]({{ 'timeline/' | relative_url }}) remains one of the most debated parts of the case.

## What the April 11 claim says

The **11 April 1933** date comes from one of the earliest widely circulated English-language presentations of Roberto Pinotti's and Alfredo Lissoni's "[Fascist UFO Files]({{ 'fascist-files/' | relative_url }})". In that account, the alleged object is described as having **"either crashed or landed"** near Magenta on 11 April 1933. Even this earliest formulation is cautious, because it does not clearly state whether the object actually crashed, made a controlled landing, or was simply found on the ground.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.metabunk.org/threads/claim-a-ufo-crashed-in-1933-italy-and-the-us-recovered-it.13282/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: metabunk.org">[Metabunk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">metabunk.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">claim a ufo crashed in 1933 italy and the us recovered it.13282</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Claim: A UFO crashed in 1933 Italy and the US Recovered it15 Dec 2023 — While the Italian UFO crash story has been circulating ar...</span></span></span>

This April date is important because it appears near the beginning of the modern public history of the case. If accepted literally, it fixes the original incident in early spring 1933 and implies that any later government activity—including investigations or recoveries—would have occurred afterwards.

However, no independently authenticated Fascist government record publicly confirms 11 April as the date of an actual incident. The date survives principally through later publications based on the anonymously supplied dossier that emerged during the 1990s rather than through archival documentation accepted by professional historians.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.metabunk.org/threads/claim-a-ufo-crashed-in-1933-italy-and-the-us-recovered-it.13282/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: metabunk.org">[Metabunk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">metabunk.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">claim a ufo crashed in 1933 italy and the us recovered it.13282</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Claim: A UFO crashed in 1933 Italy and the US Recovered it15 Dec 2023 — While the Italian UFO crash story has been circulating ar...</span></span></span>

## What the June 13 recovery date adds

The **13 June 1933** date became much more prominent in later [retellings]({{ 'retellings/' | relative_url }}).

Within the same general body of UFO literature, later summaries increasingly describe **13 June** as the day on which authorities recovered a landed unidentified craft. Some accounts also associate June with [telegrams]({{ 'telegrams/' | relative_url }}) ordering secrecy, the creation or activity of the alleged RS/33 group, and military transport of the object. Over time, these different elements became compressed into a single dramatic narrative centred on 13 June.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.wantedinrome.com/news/the-crash-that-predated-roswell-mussolinis-secret-ufo-files.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wantedinrome.com">[Wanted in Rome]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wantedinrome.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the crash that predated roswell mussolinis secret ufo files</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Wanted in RomeThe Secret Crash That Predated Roswell: Mussolini&#x27;s UFO...March 2, 2026 — 2 Mar 2026 — Italy&#x27;s Secret UFO Files: The Crash...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 2, 2026</span></span></span>

This creates an important ambiguity. If April marked the original event and June marked a later recovery or administrative response, then both dates could theoretically refer to different stages of the same alleged episode. Yet the published accounts rarely explain such a distinction explicitly. Instead, many simply replace April with June without discussing why the chronology has changed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.metabunk.org/threads/claim-a-ufo-crashed-in-1933-italy-and-the-us-recovered-it.13282/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: metabunk.org">[Metabunk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">metabunk.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">claim a ufo crashed in 1933 italy and the us recovered it.13282</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Claim: A UFO crashed in 1933 Italy and the US Recovered it15 Dec 2023 — While the Italian UFO crash story has been circulating ar...</span></span></span>

The result is that readers often encounter June as though it were the universally accepted date, even though earlier presentations had identified April.

## How later summaries choose between them

Later articles, documentaries and online discussions overwhelmingly favour **13 June** because it produces a simpler narrative.

Rather than describing multiple stages of an alleged incident, many summaries present a straightforward sequence:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--decision" markdown="1">

* unidentified craft crashes near Magenta;
* Mussolini orders secrecy;
* RS/33 investigates;
* the craft is hidden for study.

</div>

Using one memorable date makes the story easier to tell, especially after renewed public interest following David [Grusch]({{ 'grusch/' | relative_url }})'s references to an alleged 1933 Italian recovery. Popular accounts frequently repeat the June date without discussing the earlier April chronology.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.popularmechanics.com/military/research/a44466099/researcher-says-he-has-evidence-of-1933-ufo-crash-in-italy/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: popularmechanics.com">[Popular Mechanics]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">popularmechanics.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">researcher says he has evidence of 1933 ufo crash in italy</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">“Dave [Grusch] claims that the US has &#x27;quite a few&#x27; alien...Read more...</span></span></span>

## What the changing dates suggest about the evidence

The disagreement between April and June is significant because chronology is itself part of the evidence.

In historical investigations, a major event usually acquires a stable date through independent contemporary records such as newspapers, official correspondence, diaries or eyewitness testimony. The Magenta case lacks that kind of publicly verifiable chronological anchor. Instead, the competing dates depend largely on interpretations of documents whose authenticity and provenance remain disputed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.metabunk.org/threads/claim-a-ufo-crashed-in-1933-italy-and-the-us-recovered-it.13282/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: metabunk.org">[Metabunk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">metabunk.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">claim a ufo crashed in 1933 italy and the us recovered it.13282</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Claim: A UFO crashed in 1933 Italy and the US Recovered it15 Dec 2023 — While the Italian UFO crash story has been circulating ar...</span></span></span>

This does not prove that either date is impossible. It does mean that neither has been established as definitive by publicly available historical evidence. The shift from April to June therefore reflects the evolution of the modern Magenta narrative more than it demonstrates the timing of a confirmed historical event.
